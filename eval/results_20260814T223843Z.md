## Post-run investigation: the one unexpected_error
This section was added after the run above, once the single unexpected_error
(`check_permit_status('2015-4421')`, `TypeError: Failed to fetch`) was
investigated — the run's original numbers above are untouched, this is an
addendum, not a correction to what actually happened.

Re-ran `check_permit_status("2015-4421")` standalone immediately after: it
came back `found` cleanly (a real electrical permit, "Electrical service
meters only", Complete, 1309 Leander Dr). So this was a genuine one-off
network hiccup inside the browser's own `fetch()` call, not a persistent
bug or a real data problem with this permit.

However, the eval harness's classifier (`eval/classify.py`) did NOT
recognize "Failed to fetch" as a transient-network signature — it fell
through to `unexpected_error` instead of `transient`. That's a real gap,
now fixed (`"failed to fetch"` added to `_TRANSIENT_MARKERS`) for future
runs. This run's recorded category for that one call stays
`unexpected_error`, as actually observed — not retroactively reclassified,
since that would misrepresent what this specific run actually produced.

## Post-run investigation: was the street-search skew a fluke of those 6 streets?
The "Known sample limitations" section above notes all 100 candidates came
from the single broad `Keyword="electrical"` query, with the original 6
street-name queries (COTTON PATCH, LEANDER DR, CRYSTAL FALLS, BAGDAD RD,
HERO WAY, 183A TOLL) contributing zero. Checked whether that was just bad
luck on those 6 picks: pulled 8 different real streets from this run's own
candidate-pool addresses (DEERCREEK LN, OAKWOOD DR, HALSEY DR, WILLOW CREEK
DR, LAUREL GLEN BLVD, DUBLIN DR, LONDONDERRY DR, GRANITE CREEK DR — no
overlap with the original 6) and ran the identical query method
(`ExactMatch=True, PageSize=50`) against them.

Result: 7 of the 8 new streets surfaced zero electrical-typed permits too.
The one that did (DEERCREEK LN) had them at result positions 15, 20, 21,
24, 40, 41, 48 out of 50 — never in the top 15. Across all 14 street
queries tried in total (6 original + 8 new): 1/14 surfaced any electrical
permits, and even then never near the top of the ranking.

**Not a fluke.** This portal's address-scoped search relevance ranking
reliably deprioritizes electrical-type permits relative to other record
types at the same address. The broad `Keyword="electrical"` query isn't
just one option among several for building this sample — it's the
necessary one; street-name search alone essentially doesn't surface
electrical permits within a normal result-page window.
